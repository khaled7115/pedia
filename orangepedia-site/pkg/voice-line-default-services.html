1. Guidelines

  • Voice line default services are eligible for all Orange Customers (consumer & business).

  1.1 Caller ID service

  •  The Caller Line Identification Presentation (CLIP) service enables customers to identify the telephone number or name of your caller before you answer the call. With most models of phones, your caller’s name will be shown instead of their number if you have their number stored in your memory. You then have the freedom to choose which calls you want to answer.
  • Caller ID service is added by default on Siebel to all new activated lines (prepaid /postpaid).
  •  Service enables customers to identify the telephone number or name of your caller before you answer the call.
  •  The service is not to be deleted for any customer unless customer got any approval from NTRA to activate the private number service in this case service to be deleted.
  •  Caller ID service is working normally while roaming unless the other operator is having limitations on service use.
  •  The customer can divert the calls for more than one condition at the same time.
  •   Service not to be added through front liners and SR should be created instead based on customer's issue.

  1.2 Call Waiting

  • Service enables you to put a current phone call on hold and accept an incoming call or start a second call.
  • You can place a current phone call on hold and accept an incoming call or start a second call.
  • In case customer is initiating a call while the second party is already having a call, notification word “WAITING' will be displayed on the customer's mobile indicating that the 2nd party is having a call now.
    • I.e. when A party initiates a call to B party (while B part already performing a call) a notification will appear on A party handset with “waiting" word on it.
  • All windows mobiles & Mac based operating system such as the iPhone don't support the waiting notification.

  1.3 Call Forward

  • Service provides the customer with ability to forward/divert their incoming calls to any other number you choose in case of being busy, unreachable, no answer or you don't want to be disturbed.
  • Service is available to all postpaid &/or prepaid customers (business & consumer market).
  • when your mobile is busy, unreachable, not answering or you don't want to be disturbed, Orange' s Call Forwarding allows you to divert incoming calls to any other number you choose.
  • Service is added by default to all postpaid customers upon activations, In case service wasn't added normal complaint flow should be followed.
  • Prepaid customers it shall be requested through call center &/or orange shops.
  • For prepaid customers, confirmation SMS will be sent upon service addition.
  • No monthly fee is required from customer since service will be is added free of charge.
  • Service will appear only on Siebel.
  • Customers cannot forward their calls to international numbers either from Egypt or while roaming since service is only applicable with +2 numbers whether to Orange (mobile # and Short number services) or any other operator inside Egypt.
  • Customer can use the call forward service while roaming however s/he will be charged according to normal roaming rates.
  • Customer will not be able to use the call forward service while roaming in case s/he set conditions "No reachable or Unconditional Calls" but s/he can use it normally in the rest of conditions.
  • No activation SLA for the default services because the system automatically replies to the customer when he uses the * codes with the service is active.
  • If the customer needs to add call forward: 

  • The Call Forward service should be added to all customers by default; however, in case the customer is complaining about the service not being added and the service status is inactive, the CC agent should change the status (add) from Siebel as a corrective action

    1. Accordingly, in such cases, no SRs are to be sent to BO.
    2. Action will be taken from GSM Supl.>Pre Paid Default Services
    • **21*(number of your choice)# is to be used in all reachable divert cases not a specified one.
    • **004*(number of your choice)#  is to be used in unreachable cases only.
  • If the customer needs to delete call forward: All Call Forward Service should not be deleted neither from Siebel or any other channel ( i.e. automated SR on Siebel) as it is considered a basic GSM service.
  • In case the customer needs to stop call forward, s/he can cancel all diverts by using the appropriate short codes from handset, else it should be handled as a complaint.
    • ##21#.
    • #004#.

While Roaming:

  • For Postpaid Customers:
    • In case the customer diverts his/her calls on his/her free number dial, s/he will not be charged. Yet the minutes will be deducted from the free number minutes (5000 minutes).
  • For Prepaid / Control Customers:
    • Customers will be charged with the normal minute rate according to his/her profile upon receiving a forwarded call.
    • Call forward cannot be completed in case of no or insufficient credit.
    • Call will be released/dropped once customers' credit is zero.

  1.4 Call Barring

  • Call barring is a service which can be used to selectively bar outgoing and incoming calls.
  • Service is only available to pos-paid lines (business / personal) regardless the line tariff.
  • Call barring service is not available to Star credit/Mix (business / personal).
  • Service can be used to selectively bar outgoing and/or incoming calls.
  • In case anyone tried to call the customer while activating the call baring service s/he will hear the below:
    • In case calling from Orange dial no IVR heard, and call ended.
    • In case of calling from any other operation: “Sorry this subscriber has set barring of incoming calls".
  • Agent should upsell Block service.
  • The Call barring service is added through CP normal verification.
  • Customer has active call barring service on outgoing calls, NOT on sending SMS.
  • Before adding or erasing the Call barring, remove the Call forward then register it again check the type of the line it must be Star lines.
  • When the customer faces a problem using the barring codes as he forgot his Password.
  • If any customer calls to ask about barring service, After informing the customer about the details of the service and the customer wants to add or delete the service, suggest sending the codes to him through wireless SMS.
  • “Default services shouldn't be deactivated / deleted through front liner"
  • Check the International Service on the Siebel as this service is applicable only to those customers who have this service.
  • Erase & register the 4 barring from the Siebel the password will be 0000.
  • The detail of each barring must be barring by subscriber using Password instead of barring operator.

  1.5 Private number feature

  • Customers will be able to hide their mobile number when placing a call so that call recipients will not be able to identify the caller number (private number will be displayed instead of customer's mobile number).
  • Private No. is not applied in case of SMS; accordingly, customer's dial will appear normally in case of sending SMS's.
  • Private number Service can be added only to postpaid lines (Prepaid, Primo and Star credit lines are ineligible).
  • Customers who have the private number service and requested to migrate their lines will lose the service.
