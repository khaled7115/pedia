1.8 General Rules

  • Profile's fees will be deducted from the customer's main balance.
  • No proration will be applied on Alo fees or the inclusive bucket.
  • The bucket in Alo Daily tariff gives On-net minutes and can be used in making off-net calls with interchange rate (1 off-net minute = 5 minutes from the bucket).
  • Inclusive units can be used at any time of the day.
  • Alo Daily & weekly will renew automatic on renewal date in case the customer has enough credit. Their buckets are also traffic triggered in case the renewal date has not yet reached (Rolling bucket activation).
  • In case the customer doesn't have enough credit to renew the bucket, s/he will continue using the line but charged according to the out of bucket fees.
  • In case customer consumed the bucket units They by default go to Rolling bucket activation upon making a new usage. If they had deactivated Rolling bucket activation features, s/he will be charged according to the out of bucket rates.
  • In case the inclusive bucket's units were totally consumed during a call, Rolling bucket activation will be triggered, if no enough credit to renew the bucket rest of the call will be deducted from the customer's balance if any, & in case of insufficient balance call will be dropped.
  • In case the customer doesn't have GPRS service active while migration, it should be activated automatically.
  • All new profiles will be available on NPG tool for MNP customer's activation (this should be aligned by technology)
  • All new profiles should be activated same as current prepaid lines & according to the current New Line Activation process.
  • All new Tariffs monthly fees are tax inclusive and Stamp Taxes Exclusive.
  • The stamp tax should be deducted on monthly basis with the deduction of the monthly bucket fees.  It will be on the same day for customers who did not migrate.
  • Migrating customers will have the stamp tax deducted on a different date from the payment date.
  • Migration from hybrid profiles to ALO will be according to each profile

1.9 Guidelines

Starting from 8th April 2021, Prepaid & Hybrid lines will have below updates concerning their lifecycle.

  • New lifecycle will be applied for Prepaid & Hybrid profiles existing, newly activated & migrated ones.
  • Life cycle will be as below:
    • Active period: will be 90 days.
    • Inactive period: will be 10 days.
    • Deactivation period: will be 5 days.
  • Once customer enters the deactivation state s/he can re-activate the line through existing normal channels.
  • After the deactivation period customer dial will be permanently deactivated.
  • Any customer on inactive state will receive SMS to inform them to recharge in order to keep the line.
  • For Example:
    • In case customer at the deactivation state (5 Days) will be able to re-activate the line through calling 400 IVR then recharging.
    • If customer at the permanent deactivation state (after the 5 days) should visit the shop to make re-allocation if dial is still available.
  • Below action related to Prepaid & Hybrid segments will be taken on the 8th May 2021.
  • Any LTV customer didn't perform any action on their dials & exceeded 105 days of lifecycle period (active 90, inactive 10, deactivate 5) will be permanently deactivated.
  • EL Masry lines are included at this action.
  • A manual action for 3 days starting (8th May 2021)to customers who are at the old deact state (31 days) to be changed to 5 days, in case they didn't perform any recharge/ migration action.

1.11 Migration Matrix & Rolling Bucket Activation

Migration Matrix:

Updated Migration Matrix.xlsx

Rolling bucket activation

  • This feature is available for Alo daily & weekly.
  • This feature allows customers on Alo profiles to choose whether to purchase another bucket after finishing the inclusive units of the bucket or to be charged OOB.
  • After finishing bucket's units and upon making a new usage, call, SMS or internet, a new bucket will be activated with a new validity, thus making the usage always at the best rate.
  • This feature is active by default for the above profiles. i.e. upon consuming all the bucket's units, & customer initiated new consumption, new bucket will be activated automatically & customer will get new renewal date according to the new bucket's purchasing date.
  • In case this feature is active & customer doesn't have enough credit to purchase a new bucket, s/he will be charged according to the out of bucket rates.
  • Customer can request to Opt in/ out from this feature by dialing #0# --> (2) Manage tariff features --> (1) Rolling bucket activation.
  • The USSD menu will inform the customer the feature status & whether it is active or not & customer can then choose to deactivate/ activate it.
  • Customer will receive a confirmation SMS upon activating/ deactivating the feature.
  • Subscription & un-subscription from this feature is free of charges.
  • Customer can subscribe/ unsubscribe in this feature as many time as s/he wants even during the same day, & the action will be implemented on the spot.
  • If the customer chooses out of bucket option, s/he will receive a notification with the first charged OOB unit to upsell the Rolling activation functionality.
