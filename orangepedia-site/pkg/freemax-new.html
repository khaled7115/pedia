4.Group Sharing Rules:


  • Customers who will migrate or activate on FREEmax will be able to create group up to 3 members “Number of members according to which price point"
  • Each group will be led by only one owner who will be responsible for the below:
  • Adding or deleting any of the group members.
  • Renewing the monthly bucket.
  • Distributing the units among the group members.
  • Adding a member to the group should be sent by owner through Orange Max it Application or through #0#, owner will enter the number of the member then enter the amount of units to be shared.
  • Once these members join the group they will be able to benefit from the shared units.
  • Members will remain on their existing tariff and will follow the same eligibility of their existing tariffs.
  • Any member in the group will have the option to opt-out at any time without the approval of the owner through the below:
    • #212# then choose "To leave the group" 
    • or through Orange Max it Application then choose tab called "Dial & Tariff" then Option opt-out will be last one.
  • The opt-out action will be instantly & s/he lose any unconsumed allowances (units /CUG/WhatsApp).
  • Owner will be able to request specific number of units to be shared automatically with his/her members.
  • Once FREEmax customer (owner) adds & shares units to the member of his/ her group, shared units will be added on the spot without any confirmation from member side.
  • Member should be Orange dial on Hybrid or Prepaid profiles only, excluding postpaid & Business dials.
  • Owner and members are eligible to renew their main bucket normally, subscribe to extensions, GO data buckets; however owner can't share its MBs.
  • In case customer does not have sufficient credit to renew the bucket, the members will not be able to use:
    • Units allowance.
    • CUG. (Closed user group).
    • WhatsApp.
  • Once a member is removed from the group, s/he will lose the unconsumed allowances as well as the CUG and WhatsApp.
  • Once a member is added to the group by the owner that shared units, CUG, WhatsApp will follow the owner bucket expiry date
  • Member cannot be part of two groups.
  • Any customer as group owner cannot be in another group as member.
  • In-case the owner migrated out from FREEmax profile, the sharing option will be stopped and the members will remain on their normal tariffs
  • In case of owner migration upgrade from FREEmax to another, members will remain members in the group, however if owner downgraded, members will be removed from the group.
  • Shared units is a maximum percentage that is calculated from the main inclusive units.
  • If Owner made early renewal and the member still having remaining shared units will be carried over.
  • Sharing option Troublehsooting Guide. 

  • New changes will be applied to family sharing for hybrid customers.

    Replacing the current family member dial with a new family member dial will be available based on the number of allowable actions.
  • Family member will lose any shared units upon deleting/replacing his dial (remaining shared quota won't be returned to the owner (Same as current) .
  • Customers can delete and re-add the same family member during the same billing cycle, based on the number of allowable actions.
    • If party A has a members limit of 1 member, party A can add 1 member, remove the 1 member, and add 1 new member.
    • If party A has a members limit of 2 members, party A can add 2 members, remove the 2 members, and add 2 new members.
    • If party A has a member limit of 3 members, party A can add 3 members, remove the 3 members, and add 3 new members.
  • Upon deletion action, party A cannot add any new members after consuming the allowable actions till the next renewal date.

10. Tariff Features:

  • 10.1 Call Tone Service Fee Deduction:
  • Call tone subscribers will be charged from their profile units for service renewal, in case there is no enough credit in renewal date.
  • Deduction of Call tone service renewal fee will be availed by default with no opt-out.
  • The deduction from the units will be on a daily basis with the below rate:
    • Ring back tone = 10 units daily
  • Priority of deduction will be for the Main balance (Credit balance) then the inclusive units.
  • 10 units max will be deducted per day and will have one day validity.
  • If the subscriber is granted 20 days validity for example according to his balance in the first cycle, then the units deduction will start from the second cycle in the same day and every day until the remaining 10 days is recovered. If the customer recharged his balance, deduction from the main balance will take place first and then if no balance found units will be deducted with a capping of 10 units daily.
  • 10.2 Carry Over Feature:
  • FREEmax profiles will benefit from the carry over feature.
  • Feature is available by default & doesn't require any action by the customer
  • To be able to enjoy this feature, the customer has to renew the new monthly bucket on time or before renewal date 
  • Any remaining units will be carried over with the customer to the next month
  • The carried over usage is valid for one month only.
  • Example: If the payment date is 1st June and the customer did not finish the inclusive units till that day, s/he will carry over the remaining units in addition to the new bucket till 28th June.
  • In case of not having the fees to pay for the profile's monthly fees, the customer will not benefit from the carried-over units as it will expire.
  • Carried over usage has higher consumption priority over the new month's units & old extension if any.
  • Carried-over usage will be displayed on DA# 205 in Siebel (Charging Details).
  • Carried over usage will be displayed on the end-of-call notification, & customer can inquire about it using normal balance inquiry channels & fees.
  • No carryover for the Plus minutes, WhatsApp, CUG, FB.
  • Carry Over Rules For Migration:
  • When a customer migrates from a lower FREEmax tariff to a higher FREEmax tariff before the current bundle expires or on the renewal date, the remaining units from the current bundle will carry over to the new bundle, following the existing FREEmax renewal carry-over rules.
  • Only one bucket will be carried over.
    • Example: If a customer upgrades from FREEmax 80 to FREEmax 115 with 200 units remaining from the FREEmax 80 bundle, the 200 remaining units will be carried over to the new FREEmax 115 bundle.
    • If the customer then migrates to FREEmax 175 during the FREEmax 115 bundle validity, only the remaining units from the FREEmax 115 bundle will be carried over to FREEmax 175.
  • The customer will benefit from the carry-over feature while migrating from a lower FREEmax tariff to a higher FREEmax tariff before the current bundle expires or on the renewal date.
    • Meaning that the remaining units from the current bundle will carry over to the new bundle.
  • Carry-over should be applied for migration up only.
    • Meaning that the carry-over should not apply for migration down.
  • Only the remaining units from the latest 1st bundle will be carried over.
  • Only the main quota will be carried over.
  • Carry-over will take place only in case of early renewal or on-time renewal.
  • The carried-over quota will be valid until the new bundle expiry date.
  • No opt-in action is required from the customer's side.
  • Carried-over usage has higher consumption priority over the new month's units & old extension, if any.
  • Carried-over usage will be displayed on DA# 205 in Siebel (Charging Details).
  • Carried-over usage will be displayed on the end-of-call notification, & customer can inquire about it using normal balance inquiry channels & fees.

FREEmax Grace Amount Feature:

  • Orange is introducing a “Grace Amount" mechanism that allows FREEmax customers to successfully renew their bundles even if their balance is slightly insufficient due to minor deductions (e.g., stamp tax).
  • The customer will be able to renew their bucket even if their balance is slightly insufficient.
  • This update will be applied to FREEmax existing and new customers.
  • If a customer attempts to renew a FREEmax bundle and their balance is below the required price by 1 EGP, the following will be done automatically by the system:
  • The customer can benefit from the service in case of:
    • On-time renewal
    • Early renewal
    • R&R status
  • The FREEmax bundle will be renewed automatically.
  • The outstanding amount will be deducted automatically once the customer's balance is sufficient.
    • Note that if the customer does not have the full amount available at one time to deduct, the system will deduct the available balance until the full amount is covered.
  • No additional fees will be deducted from the customer's balance.
    • Meaning, if the customer borrows 1 EGP, s/he will only be required to settle 1 EGP.
  • Grace Amount will have the highest deduction priority over other outstanding amounts  (while renewing)
  • If the customer has several pending deductions, the priority of deductions should be as follows:   (while settling)
    • Stamp tax
    • Emergency credit/emergency bundle
    • Grace amount
