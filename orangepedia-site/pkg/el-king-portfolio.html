1.7.1 Regional Bonus



  • Taking El King Proposition into Regions by offering customers in selected regions in Egypt an additional data allowance when opting-in and using data within this region.
  • Customer will be able to opt-in through #12# to get the regional bonus.
  • In case customer didn't opt-in, he will not get the regional bonus
  • Regional Bonus can be used only within the region where the customer opted in (For example, customer is in Fayoum and opted in to get the regional bonus, s/he will be granted number of MBs that can be used only in Fayoum, if the customer moved to another region and wants to consume from the regional bonus, s/he has to dial #12# choose change region bonus location for EGP 5.
  • The bonus is granted with the monthly bucket renewal with validity 28 days.
  • Regional MBs bonus considered Megabytes (follow same behavior).
  • Customer has to pay the monthly fee on or one day after the renewal date in order to get the carried over Regional bonus.
  • The bonus will be granted to customers who will do early renewal, on time renewal or 5 days from entering the R&R status as part of the bucket components.
  • In case customer renewed the bucket after the 5 days, s/he will not get the regional bonus automatically & will have to opt-in in it for a fee.
  • In case customer didn't opt-in, s/he will not get the regional bonus for this month, yet in case of renewal on date or within 5 days from R&R state in the next month, customer will get the regional bonus automatically.
  • This opt-in to get the missed regional bonus will not detect the location & will maintain the previously selected location.
  • Customer can opt-in to get the missed regional bonus through #0#.
  • The region can be changed once during the bucket validity through #0#only by doing the following:
    • Opting in in the new region.
    • Paying EGP 5.
  • The regional bonus is granted and renewed automatically once per month upon bucket renewal (based on the early renewal rules) on the last requested region for free without extra fees (Changing the region is the only thing that needs paying EGP 5)
    • For example, customer is in Alexandria and is already subscribed to regional bonus, s/he moved to Cairo so s/he will not be able to consume from the regional bonus (Alexandria bonus) unless he opt-ins in Cairo region for EGP 5 through #0#& s/he will continue using the previously granted Alex bonus.
  • Upselling Notifications will be sent automatically to customers who didn't opt-in offering regional bonus & upselling “region change" for those who moved to another region and can't consume from the current bonus.
  • Regional bonus & remaining volume will be displayed on Siebel.
  • In case customer opted in regional bonus after subscribing in double your bucket offer, his/her regional bonus will be doubled.
  • In case customer didn't opt-in regional bonus, s/he will receive Push notification that directs him/her to #12# upon starting the first data session.
  • Front liners will be able to identify if the region where the consumption is done through Siebel.

1.7.4 Buck to Back Renewal

  • This feature allows customers on El King profiles to renew her/his bucket during the month after finishing the original bucket instead of being charged OOB.
  • Customer must request this renewal each time by dialing #0#, IVR, Orange Max it Application  or portal.
  • No need for the customer to finish the current bucket to request renewal.
  • Any remaining minutes from the current bucket will be carried over. Yet, carried over minutes from previous bucket will be lost.
  • Each time the bucket is renewed the customer gets new bucket validity & renewal date.
  • Back to back renewal will be with the same fees of the original bucket.
  • In case customer is unable to renew through USSD, agents should offer Orange Max it Application as alternative even if a complaint will be submitted.

1.7.5 Carry Over


  • All profiles will benefit from the carry over feature.
  • Feature is available by default & doesn't require any action by the customer. However, Customer has to pay the monthly fee on renewal date in order to get the carried over minutes /data.
  • Any remaining minutes or MBs from the main bucket will be carried over with the customer to the next month.
  • The carried over usage is valid for one month only.

Example: If the payment date is 1st of June, and the customer did not finish the inclusive minutes till that day, s/he will carry over the remaining minutes in addition to the new bucket till 1st of July.

  • Customer has to pay the monthly fee on renewal date in order to get the carried over minutes/data.
  • In case of not having the fees to pay for the profile's monthly fees, customer will not benefit from the carried over minutes/MBs. I.e. it will expire.
  • Carried over usage has higher consumption priority over the new month's minutes& old extension if any.
  • Carried over usage will be displayed as following on DA on IN as shown in the DAs table.
  • Carried over usage will be displayed on end of call notification, & customer can inquire about it using normal balance inquire channels & fees.
  • Customer will be notified with SMS with the carried over minutes& its validity in case of successful renewal on renewal date.
  • Upon unsuccessful renewal, customer will receive SMS that s/he missed the carryover due to not renewing on the renewal date.
  • WhatsApp MBs are not carried over.
 

1.7.6 Life cycle

  • El King Profiles renew every 28 days.
  • Renewal date is not fixed & will vary depending on the date the customer will have enough credit to renew the bucket in.
  • In case the customer has enough balance to renew the bucket, on the renewal date, it will renew automatically & new bucket will be added. (No need to perform a charged action, bucket will renew anyway).
  • In case there isn't enough credit for bucket renewal, line will continue normally, but bucket will not be added, customer will enter refill & renew stage, and will be charged according to the out of bucket rates.
  • Once customer recharge with enough credit for the monthly fees, it will be deducted & profile will be renewed & customer will get a new renewal date.
  • In case the customer entered the R&R state & recharged to renew the profile, renewal will take place instantly.
  • Line will follow lifetime validity rules, i.e. will stay active as long as there is an action that grants lifetime validity.
  • Lifetime validity is 105 days, with reconnecting the line through any incoming or outgoing activity (SMS, call, data ….etc.) as following:

1.7.7 Activation

  • El King Profiles will be introduced in the current all one pack.
  • After normal NLA steps, customer should call IVR 400 to choose the needed profile.
  • The pack can be activated on any of the mentioned profiles, conditioning that the customer recharge to complete the line's price.
  • This pack will have a fixed selling price of EGP 28 for all the profiles.
  • Line price is different than the pack's price.
  • Customer will be requested to recharge with an amount so that the paid amount is equal to the line's price.
  • The IVR will inform the customer by the amount that s/he needs to recharge with in order to activate the line.
  • The recharged amount will be deducted from the customer's balance to complete the activation flow.
  • The recharged amount can be accumulative till reaching the needed recharge amount.
  • In case the customer didn't recharge to complete the line's price, line will not be activated.
  • If the recharge was done with more than the credit needed, the rest will be normally available in credit main balance
  • Customer can use all available recharge methods to recharge the line before activation (scratch cards, orange money, E-recharge, portal, etc…) in addition to credit transfer.
  • Once line is activated, profile's bucket will be added. & normal life cycle will start.

1.8.2 Migration From Star Credit/ Postpaid /Eagle/ Premier

  • Migration can be requested through Orange shops/ franchises, kiosks & Vans.
  • Migration from postpaid or star credit is free of charges.
  • Any billed/ unbilled amounts should be settled before proceeding by the migration request.
  • Line will be migrated within 24 hours.
  • Any remaining credit will be moved to the new profile normally.
  • Line will be migrated directly from postpaid or star credit with no need to pass on prepaid.

1.8.3 Migration From Prepaid theough Call Center

  • Customer will be able to migrate from AL KART to El King through call center, retail, IVR, Portal, Orange Max it Application  & USSD.
  • Customer requesting migration through call center must have enough credit to migrate to the required profile
  • In case customer doesn't have enough credit, s/he should recharge in order to proceed with the migration request
  • Customer must be calling from the same dial that s/he wants to migrate.
  • Full verifications are required, noting that customer must have CSO flag & customer's ID number is a must
  • In case customer is not verified, request should be rejected & customer should be directed to the shop for ownership investigation
  • In case the customer is verified, front liner should ask her/him if s/he has a contract for the line or not
  • In case customer doesn't have contract, s/he should be advised to visit Orange shop to sign a new contract. (Noting that this point doesn't stop the migration & request should be preceded normally).

1.8.4 Change Tariff Between Hybrid Profiles 

  • Control/free/ Kalam El Ne, Panther, Dolphin & Dragon customers can change tariff to El King tariffs normally.
  • El King tariffs cannot change tariff to Dragon, Panther, Control Free/Control or Kalam EL Net profiles, instead can change tariff to FREEmax (New).
  • Change tariff between Hybrid profiles will be instant.
  • Change tariff can be done through IVR 400, USSD, call center, portal, Orange Max it Application , or Orange retail channels using WebCC.
  • In case the previous month fees are due, then customer has to pay it along with the migration fees.
  • Once the line is migrated to the new profile, customer will lose any remaining minutes/bonus credit & will start benefiting from the new profiles' inclusive package.
  • The migration date will be the new customer's payment date.
  • Customer can change tariff normally at any time before her/his payment date.

1.8.5 Migration Out

  • El King profiles subscribers can migrate to Eagle/ Premier tariffs without passing 6 months activation period.
  • Migration out of the profile to Eagle/ Premier profiles is available normally through Orange shop/franchise.
  • Migration out to prepaid will be available through IVR 400, portal, Orange Max it Application , USSD in addition to Orange shops/ franchises & CC.
  • Customer will lose any remaining minutes, MBs, or extension upon migration out from El King profiles.
  • In case of migration out to postpaid, sales agent should make sure that the customer doesn't have any due amounts.
  • In case there are any due amounts, customer should be advised to recharge to settle these amounts before migration.
  • In case the due amount was not settled before migration, the action will fail.
  • Migration out to Eagle/ Premier is free of charges.
  • Prepaid customers migrated to ElKing tariffs, on R&R state & never activated ElKing bucket, can migrate out for free to ALO.

1.9 Extensions

  • New extensions are available for El King Profiles to extend their usage & avoid being charged with the out of inclusive bucket rates.
  • Extension will grant the customer number of minutes with the conversion feature (if activated by the customer) or MBs according to the chosen one.
  • Following are the available extensions with its prices:

    • Voice extensions:  Cross-net  (1 on-net = 1 minute , 1 off-net  =  5 minutes)
      • EGP 3:   60 minutes
      • EGP 5:   160 minutes
      • EGP 10: 360 minutes
      • EGP 15: 600 minutes
    • Data Extensions:
      • EGP 5:   500 MBs
      • EGP 10: 1000 MBs
      • EGP 15: 1500 MBs
  • Customer can request this action from USSD by dialling #0# & go to (Account Management, Additional Features, then Extension).
  • Extension validity will follow the original bucket's validity & end date.
  • Customer can add as many extensions as they want.
  • Customer can add another extension even the old one is not finished, as the new extension minutes will be added to the remaining minutes/MBs and no change will be done for the extension expiry date (it always has the expiry of the current active bucket).
  • Customer can't subscribe to extension while R&R state.
  • Customers will not be able to use the minutes/MBs in their extension during the inactive period, yet once customer renews, the customer will be able to use the extension for the remaining validity.
  • Remaining Minutes/MBs from the extension can be carried over.
  • Consumption priority is for the older minutes/MBs either the inclusive bucket or the extension minutes.

Example: customer subscribed to El King Profile his bucket renews monthly on the 15th of the month,s/he added extension on 30/5. Yet, he has remaining minutes & MBs in his main bucket; s/he will keep consuming from the main bucket till it is fished then start to consume from the extension. Upon main bucket renewal remaining minutesfrom the extension will be carried over.

  • Customer can check extensions consumption through Orange Max it Application , under “My consumption" Section.
