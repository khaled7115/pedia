 Starting 5/6/2010:

§  Orange will enable the “lifetime validity" feature (LTV) on the Business Smart tariff.
§  In case a Business Smart line does not recharge, or initiates any charged action (voice call, SMS, MMS "stopped", data consumption, etc…) in 3 consecutive months (93 days), the line will enter In-activity period (suspension period) for 10 days grace period.
§  During the 10 days grace period the customer will not be able to make calls however, s/he will receive calls normally.
§  In case the customer doesn't recharge or send a SMS during the grace period the line will be permanently deactivated. In other words the customer can only reactivate his/her line by recharging or sending a SMS, s/he will not be able to do any other action.
§  Keeping the line status active for 3 month (before entering the grace period), customer must perform any chargeable action as follows:
-       Voice call.
-       Send SMS.
-       Send MMS Stopped.
-       Data consumption.
-       Charging.
§  In the day 94 (the day after the 93 days and before entering the 10 days grace period) the only action that will reactivate the line is recharging.
§  After entering the grace period, only sending SMS or recharging will reactivate the line. Incase the customer performed any of the above actions then the 3 month period will be reset starting from the charged action date.
For the current existing business smart subscribers:
§  Orange will start counting the 3 month for the current Business smart customers starting from the launch date of the Lifetime time validity feature 5/6/2010.

1-What is “Orange Business"?

-Sir/Madam, “Orange Business" is a comprehensive service that customizes solutions to you/your company based on your needs and requirements. A Business executive from Orange will visit you at your convenience to discuss with you the different options and solutions customized specially for your organization.
2-What are the benefits that my company would get from “Orange Business"?
-Sir/Madam, “Orange Business" provides several benefits to our customers such as:Cutting Edge Technology, through our new existing Value Added Services such as: Numbering plans, Twin line.Best Business Rates in Egypt, through Value for Money.
Dedicated Business Customer Care Line, the 250 team.
Customization of Business solutions.

3-What are the “Customized Business Solutions"?
-Sir/Madam, Orange Business Solutions offers unique solutions according to your company's requirements such as:
Connection Fee Reduction Program.
Monthly Fee Reduction Program.
Budget Based Program or “Budget Master".
Flexible Payment Methods.
Business Bundles.
Innovative Mobile Management Services (Free), every line gets: CLIP, Call Waiting/Call Hold, Call Forward, Voicemail, SMS, Monthly Free minutes, Usage Based Tariffs, Dedicated Customer Care, Dedicated Business Team, Extensive Coverage & Roaming, The Connector of Fixed Wireless Terminal.

 
4-What are the Business Tariffs?
-Sir/Madam, when the business executive visits you, and according to the number of lines in the name of your company and if you choose any other services that Orange offers, he will be able to offer you the best price. The price per minute is the same as the Classic (EGP 0.45 peak, EGP 0.35 off-peak & EGP 0.20 Orange to Orange & EGP 0.30 from Mobile to Mobile).

5-What is the price of the line today?
-Sir/Madam, NOW the business line is EGP 50 in all Orange shops and authorized agents. But this price could change during business offers periods SO you better check with you account manager or the 250 customer service team about prices before visiting the shops

6-What are the flexible billing services?
-Single bill vs. multiple bills, which will allow your organization to receive one consolidated bill with all the company lines.
Expanding network of banks and Orange centers to process payment, which allows you now to pay your invoice in different banks such as National Bank, Banque Misr, etc…
Direct Debit, which is an automatic payment through banking direct debit services.
Monthly itemized bill, which enables you to monitor your monthly mobile communication expenditure.
Office cash collection, now you can pay your bill from the convenience of your office.
Office cash collection, office check collection now you can pay your bill from the convenience of your office. E-payment

7-Does Orange Business offer international and roaming with no deposit?
-Yes, Orange offers international and roaming access with zero deposit, Depending on the account eligibility, due bills and history.
