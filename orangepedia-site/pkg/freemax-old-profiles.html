3. Tariffs Guidelines:


  • FUP is applied on WhatsApp allowance and Plus minutes calls, yet agent shouldn't communicate the FUP, unless the customer insists to know it.(check WhatsApp & Plus sections)
  • For social media team, FUP replies should be provided in a private message.
  • All tariffs will have “Plus minutes" by Default (except FREEmax Old 37) and can change to “Plus Social".
  • The “Plus" will give the customer up to double his/her profile units for plus social, and calls for orange dials for free for plus minutes subscribers.
  • Customer can get only one “Plus" at a time.
  • Call tone subscribers will be charged from their profile unites for service renewal, in case no enough money in renewal date 10 units will be deducted per day.
  • Upon purchasing a new line, customer is required to call 400 to activate it.
  • FREEmax Old portfolio will be grouped in the current all in one pack (new pack design will be provided later).
  • Upon activating the line through 400 IVR, customers can choose to activate the line on the desired tariff/profile following the current all in one back rules.
  • FREEmax Old profiles are available for new selling & migration from prepaid, star credit, postpaid or existing control, Dolphin, Panther, Dragon & El King profiles.
  • Migration to FREEmax Old portfolio is valid through Orange sales channels (shops & franchises), In addition to call center & IVR 400/ USSD (in case of migration from prepaid).
  • FREEmax Old portfolio profiles will use prepaid IVR 400, Orange Max it Application , and Orange Portal for all required actions (recharge, general info, balance inquire, adding services etc.…).
  • Profiles will be displayed with the following name on Siebel as Control 35, FREEmax Old 30, 70 & 80.
  • FREEmax Old portfolio will be handled through C&C teams & communicated to the customer as postpaid tariffs.
  • FREEmax Old portfolio is not available for MNP customers' activation anymore.
  • FREEmax Old portfolio Tariffs monthly fees are tax, and Stamp Taxes Exclusive.
  • FREEmax Old tariffs will be handled by C&C call center.
  • Call to 110 for EGP 0.50.
  • Customer will be able to subscribe in all GO and Go  internet packages, extensions & renewable extensions.
  • In case the customer doesn't have the GPRS service active while migration, it should be activated automatically.
  • Upon dialing code #74# customers will be directed to Hybrid Platform #20#

5. Tariff Features:

  • 5.1 Plus Social & Plus minutes:
  • Plus Social & Plus minutes feature is valid for all FREEmax Old profiles except control 35.
  • Plus minutes will be built-in in all tariffs with zero fees (except for control 35).
  • Plus Social enable the customer to use double of his/her bucket units to make data traffic on social sites.
  • Plus minutes will allow the customer to make free calls to orange numbers after consuming his/her bucket's units.
  • Plus minutes will allow the customer to make free calls to orange numbers after consuming his/her bucket's units with the below FUP:
    • FREEmax Old 30: 400 minutes.
    • FREEmax Old 70: 850 minutes.
    • FREEmax Old 80: 1500 minutes
  • Social media websites are WhatsApp, Facebook, Facebook messenger, Instagram, snapchat, and twitter.
  • No Opt-out from Plus Social or Plus minutes, yet s/he can change from one of them to another for free through #22#.
  • Customer can change from Plus Social to Plus minutes or vice versa only once per month (28 bucket validity).
  • Customer will not be able to switch between plus options before 28 days even if s/he made back to back renewal.
  • Customers can't subscribe to Plus Social & Plus minutes at the same time, yet s/he can only switch between both.
  • In case customer switched from Plus minutes to plus social, s/he will lose the remaining plus minutes, yet in case s/he switched from plus social to plus minutes while having remaining units, s/he will benefit from plus minutes calls normally.
  •  In case the customer consumed all his/her units, s/he will be able to switch normally between plus options (one time during bucket validity 28 days), yet s/he will not get the chosen plus option allowance.
  • Example: customer chose plus social option, consumed all his/her units, and switched to plus minutes, s/he will not get plus minutes allowance for this life cycle.
  • Customer will not be able to switch between switch between plus options before 28 days even if s/he made back to back renewal.
  • Plus minutes will be added to DA 212 on Seibel.
  • Plus social will not duplicate customer's units on Seibel, yet it'll double customer's usage to social media websites.
  • There is no carryover for the Plus minutes.
  • 5.2 Non-stop WhatsApp:
  • Customer will enjoy continuous WhatsApp usage as long as s/he is within the bucket validity duration.
  • Consuming from WhatsApp allowance MBs start when the customer finishes all his/her inclusive units.
  • Once the allowance is over, customer will be charged with out of bucket rates for EGP 0.25 per MB.
  • WhatsApp allowance/FUP is as following:
    • FREEmax Old 37: 50 MBs.
    • FREEmax Old 30: 50 MBs.
    • FREEmax Old 70: 200 MBs.
    • FREEmax Old 80: 200 MBs.
  • 5.3 Call tone service fee deduction:
  • Call tone subscribers will be charged from their profile unites for service renewal, in case no enough money in renewal date.
  • Deduction of Call tone service renewal fee will be availed by default with no opt-out.
  • The deduction from the units will be on a daily basis with the below rate:
    • Ring back tone = 10 units daily
  • Priority of deduction will be for the Main balance (Credit balance) then the inclusive units.
  • 10 units max will be deducted per day and will have one day validity.
  • If the subscriber is granted 20 days validity for example according to his balance in the first cycle, then the units deduction will start from the second cycle in the same day and every day until the remaining 10 days is recovered. If the customer recharged his balance, deduction from the main balance will take place first and then if no balance found units will be deducted with a capping of 10 units daily.
  • 5.4 Carry over:
  • All FREEmax Old profiles will benefit from the carry over feature.
  • Feature is available by default & doesn't require any action by the customer.
  • To be able to enjoy this feature, customer has to renew the new monthly bucket on time, before renewal date or maximum on the 1st day after the bucket validity expires.
  • Any remaining units will be carried over with the customer to the next month.
  • The carried over usage is valid for one month only.
  • Example: If the payment date is 1 June and the customer did not finish the inclusive units till that day, s/he will carry over the remaining units in addition to the new bucket till 1 July.
  • In case of not having the fees to pay for the profile's monthly fees, customer will not benefit from the carried over units as it will be expired.
  • Carried over usage has higher consumption priority over the new month's units.
  • Carried over usage will be displayed as following on DA on IN as shown in the Das table.
  • Carried over usage will be displayed on end of call notification, & customer can inquire about it using normal balance inquire channels & fees.
  • Customer will be notified with SMS with the carried over units & its validity in case of successful renewal on renewal date.
  • Upon unsuccessful renewal, customer will receive SMS that s/he missed the carryover due to not renewing on the renewal date.
  • No carryover for the Plus minutes.
  • Carry Over Rules For Migration:
  • When a customer migrates from a lower FREEmax tariff to a higher FREEmax tariff before the current bundle expires or on the renewal date, the remaining units from the current bundle will carry over to the new bundle, following the existing FREEmax renewal carry-over rules.
  • Only one bucket will be carried over.
    • Example: If a customer upgrades from FREEmax 80 to FREEmax 115 with 200 units remaining from the FREEmax 80 bundle, the 200 remaining units will be carried over to the new FREEmax 115 bundle.
    • If the customer then migrates to FREEmax 175 during the FREEmax 115 bundle validity, only the remaining units from the FREEmax 115 bundle will be carried over to FREEmax 175.
  • The customer will benefit from the carry-over feature while migrating from a lower FREEmax tariff to a higher FREEmax tariff before the current bundle expires or on the renewal date.
    • Meaning that the remaining units from the current bundle will carry over to the new bundle.
  • Carry-over should be applied for migration up only.
    • Meaning that the carry-over should not apply for migration down.
  • Only the remaining units from the latest 1st bundle will be carried over.
  • Only the main quota will be carried over.
  • Carry-over will take place only in case of early renewal or on-time renewal.
  • The carried-over quota will be valid until the new bundle expiry date.
  • No opt-in action is required from the customer's side.
  • Carried-over usage has higher consumption priority over the new month's units & old extension, if any.
  • Carried-over usage will be displayed on DA# 205 in Siebel (Charging Details).
  • Carried-over usage will be displayed on the end-of-call notification, & customer can inquire about it using normal balance inquiry channels & fees.

  • 5.5 Converting Carry Over to discount:
  • FREEmax customers can choose to convert the carried over units to monetary discount on the next monthly fees.
  • Customer can replace each remaining 100 units by 1 EGP discount from the coming monthly fees.
  • The default option will be the normal carry over option, & if customer wants to change it to the new feature s/he should change it from #0#.
  • Customer needs to perform this change only once & it will be active for lifetime unless s/he decided to change it again.
  • Customer will be able to change the options through nay channels (USSD, IVR, portal& Orange Max it Application ) for free.
  • The changing value will be only per 100 units.
  • The remaining fraction if any will be carried over to the next month with the same validity date of the new bucket.
  • Example: customer has 130 units remaining, if the increments are in 100 units and 1 EGP = 100 units, this customer will get EGP 1 discount and 30 units will be carried over normally to the next month.
  • Any carried over units from the previous month will not be counted within the units that can be redeemed as discount.
  • Upon renewal, the customer should have the final discounted fees value (not the full fee) in his credit to renew the bucket.
  • Customer can benefit from discount (converting the remaining units to discount) if he renewed the bucket any time till the renewal date.
  • In case the customer changes the option, it will be applied starting the nearest next renewal.
  • There is no capping /maximum for the discount.
  • Remaining units option chosen by the customer will be reflected on Seibel as following:
  • Accumulator 344 “remaining units":
    • 76/ not found à carryover option
    • 75 value à discount option (shylock)
    • Discount value will be displayed in DA# 285.
  • 5.6 Life Cycle:
  • FREEmax Old profiles renew every 28 days.
  • Renewal date is not fixed & will vary depending on the date the customer will have enough credit to renew the bucket in.
  • In case there's enough credit in the renewal date, renewal will be done within 1 hour.
  • In case there isn't enough credit for bucket renewal, line will continue normally, but bucket will not be added & customer will enter refill & renew stage, and will be charged according to the out of bucket rates.
  • Once customer recharge with enough credit for the monthly fees, it will be deducted, profile will be renewed & customer will get a new renewal date.
  • In case the customer entered the R&R state, and recharged to renew the profile, renewal will take place within 1 hour.
  • No partial payment will be deducted; recharges will add normal credit balance until it's enough for full monthly fees, and then the new bucket is activated.
  • In case customer's credit is less than the monthly fee by “EGP 1" profile will renew normally & it'll be deducted from the next recharge.
  • Customer will receive SMS upon renewal to inform her/him  that (renewal done and you are short EGP X) once the customer recharges and we deduct the due amount, then the customer should receive another SMS that informs him that we deducted the due amount EGP x)
  • Tariffs' debit amount will be displayed in DA# 202.
  • 5.7 Back to back renewal:
  • This feature allows customers on FREEmax Old profiles to renew their bucket during the month after finishing the original bucket instead of being charged OOB.
  • Customer must request this renewal each time by dialing #0#, IVR, Orange Max it Application  or portal.
  • In order to renew his/her bucket through Orange Max it Application , from Dashboard customer should click the dolphin icon, and select Tariff features.
  • Customers can renew their bucket directly through using #18# code.
  • No need for the customer to finish the current bucket to request renewal.
  • Any remaining units from the current bucket will be carried over. Yet, carried over units from previous bucket will be lost.
  •  Each time the bucket is renewed the customer gets new bucket validity & renewal date.
  • Back to back renewal will be with the same fees of the original bucket

How to Manage My Account

7. Line Modification:

  • 7.1 Activation:
  •  FREEmax profiles will be introduced in the current all one pack.
  • After normal NLA steps, customer should call IVR 400 to choose the needed profile.
  • The pack can be activated on any of the mentioned profiles, conditioning that the customer recharge to complete the line's price.
  • This pack will have a fixed selling price of EGP 28 for all the profiles.
  • Line price is different than the pack's price.
  • Customer will be requested to recharge with an amount so that the paid amount is equal to the line's price.
  • The IVR will inform the customer by the amount that s/he needs to recharge with in order to activate the line.
  • The recharged amount will be deducted from the customer's balance to complete the activation flow.
  • The recharged amount can be accumulative till reaching the needed recharge amount.
  • In case customer didn't recharge to complete the line's price, line will not be activated.
  • If the recharge was done with more than the credit needed, the rest will be normally available in credit main balance
  • Customer can use all available recharge methods to recharge the line before activation (scratch cards, orange money, E-recharge, portal, etc…) in addition to credit transfer.
  • Once line is activated, profile's bucket will be added. & normal life cycle will start. (STOPPED)
  • 7.2 Migrations:
  • 7.2.1 Migration In:
  • You don't have enough credit to migrate, please recharge your credit within 2 days to be able to migrate
  • Hybrid customer in R&R state (Control, FREEmax Old, El King, Dolphin & Dragon) can request to migrate to Freemax with no enough credit. (Credit deduction priority will be for the migration request not for monthly bucket renewal)
  • Customer will request the migration through normal channels 400 IVR or USSD or Orange Max it Application  or Portal.
  • Once the customer requests the migration a flag on Siebel will be added for 48 hrs.
  • Migration will be done once the customer has sufficient credit for the requested migration tariff.
  • In case customer didn't have enough credit after the 48 hrs flag will be removed and customer can renew his/her current tariff.
  • Below SMS will be sent to the customer upon migration request if no enough credit for migration:
  • لا يوجد رصيد كافي للتحويل الآن، برجاء شحن رصيدك خلال يومين للتحويل
  • Agent can check the flag through Siebel on Offer ID 10215.
  • Arabic Script:
  • حضرتك لما بتطلب التحويل من 400 أو #0# أو #100# أو  أبلكيشن اورنچ  أو موقع اورنچ لأي من أنظمة FREEmax أو باقات الكينج في حالة أنك بتتحاسب بأسعار خارج الباقة (زي مثلا لو حضرتك علي أنظمة FREEmax أو الكينج أو كنترول بلس أو دولفين أو دراجون)، لو معاك رصيد كافي للتحويل هتتحول تلقائيا من غير خصم الرصيد في تجديد باقة نظامك الحالي.
  • بنتيح لحضرتك 48 ساعة فرصة عشان تحول بالرصيد ده بدون خصمه في تجديد باقة نظامك الحالي.
  • بعد ال48 ساعة لو الرصيد كافي بيبقي في الحالة دي لتجديد باقة نظام.
  • 7.2.2.Migration Out:
  • FREEmax Old subscribers can migrate to Eagle tariffs without passing 4 months activation period.
  • Migration out of the profile to Eagle profiles is available normally through Orange shop/franchise
  • Migration out to prepaid will be available through IVR 400, USSD in addition to Orange shops/ franchises & CC
  • Customer will lose any remaining minutes, MBs, or voice extension upon migration out from Dolphin
  • In case of migration out to postpaid, sales agent should make sure that the customer doesn't have any due amounts.
  • In case there are any due amounts, customer should be advised to recharge to settle these amounts before migration.
  • In case the due amount was not settled before migration, the action will fail.
  • Migration out to Eagle is free of charges.
  • Prepaid customers who migrated to FREEmax Old profiles & never activated a bucket will be able to migrate back to available prepaid profiles with 5 EGP migration fees.

8. Hints & Tips:

  • Short numbers calls are deducted from the subscriber credit (main balance) not from the bucket.
  • Customer will be able to inquire about the remaining inclusive bucket (voice) through 400, USSD.
  • Customer can also know the bucket's remaining units & renewal date from #0# for free.
  • Remaining amounts are communicated in total remaining (sum of remaining units from the bucket + carried over amount)
  • In case customer inquired about the remaining amount/ renewal date, s/he will receive SMS that include this data.
  • Customers will receive free end of call notification after every charged call from bucket, but customer is only charged for the notification when it is from a call from the Main Account.
  • Renewal date will be displayed on end of call notification.
  • FREEmax Old profiles lines will follow prepaid activation process , (according to Control rules) for more details about NLA process press here
  • FREEmax Old profiles are eligible for Salefny service & Rasseny.
  • FREEmax Old profiles are ineligible for the daily SMS bucket.
  • Freeze your line service is available for these profiles.
  • In case customer is having wrong charges dispute, normal prepaid handling shall take place.
  • Call details inquiries handling will follow prepaid handling.

10. Life Cycle:

  • New lifecycle will be applied for Prepaid & Hybrid profiles existing, newly activated & migrated ones.
  • Life cycle will be as below:
  • Active period: will be 90 days.
  • Inactive period: will be 10 days.
  • Deactivation period: will be 5 days.
  • Line will follow lifetime validity rules (105 days) as following: Once customer enters the deactivation state s/he can re-activate the line through existing normal channels.
  • After the deactivation period customer dial will be permanently deactivated.
  • Any customer on inactive state will receive SMS to inform them to recharge in order to keep the line.
  • For Example:
  • Below action related to Prepaid & Hybrid segments will be taken today 8th May 2021.
  • Any LTV customer didn't perform any action on their dials & exceeded 105 days of lifecycle period (active 90, inactive 10, deactivate 5) will be permanently deactivated.
  • EL Masry lines are included at this action.
  • A manual action for 3 days starting today (8th May 2021)to customers who are at the old deact state (31 days) to be changed to 5 days, in case they didn't perform any recharge/ migration action.
  • In case customer at the deactivation state (5 Days) will be able to re-activate the line through calling 400 IVR then recharging.
  • If customer at the permanent deactivation state (after the 5 days) should visit the shop to make re-allocation if dial is still available.
