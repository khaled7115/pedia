1. Introduction:

  • As NTRA request & Orange continuous efforts to support special needs customers, A special discount on FREEmax (New) monthly fees will be available for these special customers.
  • Starting 5-April-26 Orange will launch a new benefit for customers with a “Special Needs" ID. Eligible customers will receive one FREEmax dial with waived line fees, in addition to the existing 50% discount on FREEmax bundle fees.

2. Guidelines:

  • Special Needs offer will be valid starting from 09/05/2021.
  • Orange is availing new offer for special needs customers where they can benefit from 50% discount on FREEmax (New) monthly fees.
  • Offer is permanent which means customer can benefit as long as line is active.
  • Every special customer can get only 1 discount offer per NID.
  • Discount is on the monthly bucket renewal fees only (migration fees will be paid in full)
  • Below table represents every profile fee after the discount:

  • Customer can benefit from the offer if it's a new line, existing, migrating to FREEmax profiles.
  • Customer will not lose the offer if s/he downgrades or upgrade within FREEmax (New) profiles.
  • In order to benefit from the offer customer should visit Orange sales channels in order to submit his/her disability documents, also dial should be registered with his/her name & to provide the national ID with the below documents:
  • Disability documents:
  • Disabled ID & disability certificate OR Integrated services card for the disabled and they should be valid.
  • After customer visit to the shop & uploading above documents on Siebel, sales agent will create SR#11527 on Siebel which will run to personal activation team for validation.
  • Once documents are validated activation agent will create SR to add the offer within 1 hr.
  • Special needs offer documents can be submitted through any sales channel.
  • Customer can benefit from Special Needs Offer along with Hybrid platform discount/double quota offers.
  • Starting 5-April-26 Customers with a valid “Special Needs" ID are entitled to one FREEmax dial with free line fees, alongside the current 50% discount on bundle fees.
  • Customers already benefiting from the 50% Special Needs offer on another dial are not eligible for the free line.
  • If a customer has an existing Orange dial without the 50% discount, the shop agent should advise them to activate the offer on their current line.
  • If the customer does not have a dial or requests the offer on a new one, the sales agent should offer a new dial with waived line fees.
  • In case of migration, the normal migration fees will apply.
  • Each customer is eligible for only one Special Needs discount per National ID. 
  • Noting that customers can receive the FREEmax dial with waived line fees through Orange national shops only
